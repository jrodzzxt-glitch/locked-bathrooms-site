Locked Bathrooms in Schools

Understanding the issue

Problem Effects Solutions

Should schools lock bathrooms?

The Problem

Schools lock bathrooms due to vaping, vandalism, and skipping class, but it can affect students who genuinely need access.

Effects

Health

Holding it too long can cause discomfort.

Stress

Students feel anxiety asking to use restrooms.

Frustration

Students feel punished for others' actions.

Solutions

  • Better supervision
  • Restroom monitoring
  • Student accountability systems

Made for awareness about school bathroom policies
